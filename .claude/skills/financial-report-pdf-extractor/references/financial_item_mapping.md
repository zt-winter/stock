## Accounting Standard Specific Mappings

### HKFRS/IFRS Specific
- "Statement of financial position" = Balance sheet
- "Statement of profit or loss" = Income statement
- "Statement of cash flows" = Cash flow statement
- "Right-of-use assets" (HKFRS 16) = Previously "Leased assets"
- "Contract assets/liabilities" (HKFRS 15) = Revenue-related items

### US GAAP Specific
- "Balance sheet" = Statement of financial position
- "Income statement" = Statement of operations
- "Property, plant and equipment" = Fixed assets
- "Accounts receivable" = Trade receivables
- "Accounts payable" = Trade payables

### Chinese Accounting Standards (CAS)
- "固定資產" = Property, plant and equipment
- "流動資產" = Current assets
- "負債" = Liabilities
- "所有者權益" = Equity
- "利潤表" = Income statement
- "現金流量表" = Cash flow statement

## Extraction Tips

1. **Handle bilingual labels**: Check both English and Chinese columns
2. **Normalize variations**: Map all variations to standardized names
3. **Preserve notes**: Keep note references (numbers in parentheses) for traceability
4. **Maintain hierarchy**: Keep section headers (Non-current assets, Current assets, etc.)
5. **Handle totals separately**: Identify and properly format total lines

## Standardized Output Format

When generating standardized output, use this hierarchy:

```

├── Assets
│   ├── Non-current Assets
│   │   ├── Property, Plant & Equipment

│   │   └── ...
│   └── Current Assets
│       ├── Inventories
│       ├── Trade & Other Receivables

│       └── ...
├── Liabilities
```
